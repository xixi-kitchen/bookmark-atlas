My canvas has not appeared on another device.

Use the same Chrome account and expected profile on both devices. Enable Chrome Sync for extensions, settings and bookmarks and check both devices are online. Eligible data syncs when Chrome Sync and quota allow; Chrome does not guarantee a delivery time. Returning to an extension page checks for newer state; visible pages also check every 30 seconds as a fallback.

Native bookmarks and the canvas use different storage paths. A synced bookmark does not mean all canvas changes or attachments have arrived. Keep a full backup first.

Why are images or attachments missing?

Images and binary attachments stay on the device where they were added, outside Chrome Sync. Use a full JSON backup/export for a complete copy and keep the original until the imported result is checked.

A webpage or video will not display.

Use the Embed tool, not bookmark import. Follow the step-by-step instructions.

Bilibili homepages load as webpages. BV / av / player videos use the official player with autoplay off; the active toolbar can also show an original video page. b23.tv short links are not resolved. YouTube supports videos, Shorts, live streams and playlists, not its homepage as a video.

Sites can prohibit embedding through X-Frame-Options or CSP frame-ancestors. Sign-in, region, age and copyright restrictions still apply and are not bypassed. The iframe sandbox restricts top-level navigation so it cannot directly replace the extension page; provider popups, requests or downloads may still occur.

Use the embed toolbar to open a regular tab if needed. Request another website adaptation with a public URL, Chrome/extension versions and reproduction steps. Requests need assessment; not every site can be supported or delivered to a fixed date.

Why did capture keep me on the current page?

Turning off Open canvas after adding keeps you on the current page. The native bookmark is saved under Other bookmarks / Bookmark Atlas. Insertion stays queued locally until a canvas opens and saves successfully.

Do I need an AI account or key?

No. Bookmarks, search, drawing and manual organization work without AI. The offline example sends nothing; real organization uses your chosen compatible service after explicit approval of selected metadata.

Configuration and credentials stay encrypted locally, outside sync and backups, and are not an OS keychain. Model tests may also incur charges. See the AI steps and privacy policy.

Which browsers are officially supported?

The public release supports Chrome. Edge and Safari are not officially supported; development builds or compatibility checks do not establish store availability.

Report a problem or request a site

Open a GitHub Issue with your Chrome and extension versions, a public URL, steps, and expected versus actual behavior. Redact screenshots and never upload credentials, API keys, tokens, full backups or private bookmarks.
