Explore web embeds

Put the web on your canvas.

This is more than saving a link. Excalidraw’s Embed tool places an interactive webpage or player on the canvas, with space around it for notes, drawings, and references.

Learn how to add an embed
Bilibili
Browse the homepage; BV, av and player links use the official player with autoplay off by default. Original video URLs can also switch to the full page.
YouTube
Videos, Shorts, live streams and playlists resolve to official embeds. Use a content URL, not the YouTube homepage.

Website embedding, sign-in and playback rules still apply. Sandboxing restricts direct replacement of the extension page; unsupported links can open in a new tab. Request another site through Issues.

Clear about the rest.

Read the privacy policy

Your workspace stays close

The full canvas stays local. Eligible lightweight canvas data can sync through the same Chrome account when sync is available and quota allows; Chrome does not guarantee a delivery time. Attachments stay on the device where you add them. Export a full backup to keep a complete copy.

AI, only when you choose it

Use your own service, approve selected metadata, and preview before applying. Saved credentials are encrypted locally, excluded from sync and backups, and are not an OS keychain. Provider fees and policies apply.
